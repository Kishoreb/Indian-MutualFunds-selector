### rules
State: observed
- Summary:
  - Mapping a holding to an AMFI scheme: first by ISIN. If the ISIN is not in AMFI's file, a name match against Direct-Growth schemes is tried; it must reach 90% similarity and lead the runner-up by 2 points, otherwise the holding is unmapped. A name match is flagged "verify" in the output.
  - A Rs 0 valuation gives REVIEW ("appears to be wound up or closed") before anything else. An unmapped holding or a failed NAV download gives INSUFFICIENT DATA.
  - A last NAV older than 10 days gives a red flag and REVIEW. Less than 3 years of history gives INSUFFICIENT DATA, unless the NAV is stale.
  - Three kinds of evidence, each with levels: absolute (3-year CAGR below the 6.5% risk-free rate is red; else 5-year below it is amber), benchmark (3-year excess return at or below minus 2 points a year is red, below 0 is amber; beating the benchmark in under 30% of rolling windows is red, under 45% amber, at least 60% green), and peers (percentile at or below 25 is red, below 50 is amber, at least 50 is green; needs at least 8 ranked peers; a max drawdown more than 10 points deeper than the peer median is amber).
  - Verdict counts evidence types, not flags: REVIEW when the NAV is stale, or red in 2 or more types, or red in 1 and amber in 2 others; CONTINUE - WATCH when red in 1 type or amber in 2; otherwise CONTINUE.
  - Confidence is High with benchmark and peers, Medium with one of them, Low with neither (judged on absolute returns only). Only categories in `PEER_CATEGORY_KEYWORDS` (large, mid, small, multi, flexi, ELSS, focused, value, contra, dividend yield, several hybrid kinds, arbitrage) get peers. Index, fund-of-funds, sectoral, thematic and most debt funds do not.
  - Peer ranking uses `shortlist.score_and_rank` with the weights in `weights.ini`; no hard filters are applied; metrics this screener does not compute (portfolio quality, downside capture, quartile consistency) are missing and the other weights are rescaled; a fund under the coverage floor gets no peer entry.
  - Portfolio notes that never change the verdict: a single fund above 15% of the portfolio, 3 or more of your funds in one category (overlap), several folios of one fund, a Regular-plan ISIN, an IDCW option.

### Conclusion
- Does: Tells the owner, for each fund they hold, whether to keep holding it, watch it or review it, with the reasons, how much of the portfolio it is, and where funds overlap.
- Rules: as in the rules facet; every threshold is in `REVIEW_RULES` (`mf_holdings_screener.py:64-80`).
- On failure: four stop conditions with a message; per-fund failures become INSUFFICIENT DATA.
- Test result: well tested for the verdict rules, the peer ranking and the end-to-end happy path with several failure cases (stale, zero value, young, unmapped).
- Tests check: row-level verdicts, confidence, notes, totals and sheet names in a written workbook, plus rule-by-rule verdict cases.
- Known gaps: No test calls `load_navall` (download, cache, fallback) or `read_holdings` directly, and no test drives the four `sys.exit` stop conditions; no test asserts the peer cap at `MAX_FUNDS_TO_SCAN`. The `.xlsx` run asserts only the peer columns and one verdict value.

## Supporting code

- `parse_navall` handles AMFI's old layout (plan and option inside the name) and the current layout (separate Plan and Option columns); both are tested.
- `AmfiIndex.match_name` normalises names (drops words like "direct", "growth", "plan" and a scheme code printed in front of the name) and compares with `difflib`.
- `write_xls` and `write_xlsx` build the workbooks; the verdict column is coloured (REVIEW rose, WATCH yellow, CONTINUE green, INSUFFICIENT DATA grey).
- The input file is expected to come from `cdsl_holdings_to_xls.py` (E006, read in S06). The docstring says so.
