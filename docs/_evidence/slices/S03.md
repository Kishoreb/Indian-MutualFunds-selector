### rules
State: observed
- Summary:
  - Each metric is turned into a 0-100 percentile score among the funds being ranked: `(average rank - 1) / (n - 1) * 100`; a single fund gets 50; tied values share the average. Under `zscore` the composite uses z-scores instead, and 0 when there is no spread. The 0-100 `final_score_0_100` is always percentile-based.
  - `downside_capture` and `beta` are lower-is-better and are sign-flipped. `max_drawdown` is stored negative, so a smaller fall is already the higher number.
  - Policy `renormalize_available` (the shipped setting): a fund's score is the weighted average over the metrics it has. A fund whose measured weight is below the coverage floor (70%) is `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` and gets no rank. Policy `average`: a missing metric counts as an average score.
  - Ranks run 1 to n among ranked funds; ties on score break by scheme code, so the output is stable. Group confidence is `LOW` when fewer than `min_group_size` (8) funds are ranked.
  - The weights file is checked hard: it must exist, have both sections, a known policy and method, every metric (the two optional ones count as 0), no unknown metric, no negative or non-numeric weight, and a total of 100 (tolerance 0.01). Expense ratio is deliberately not a metric.
  - Shipped weights (`weights.ini:34-52`): 1-year CAGR 5, 3-year CAGR 10, 5-year CAGR 15, Sharpe-style ratio 5, Sortino 5, rolling positive 10, SIP XIRR 5, max drawdown 10, benchmark excess 10, rolling beat 10, portfolio quality 5, downside capture 0, quartile consistency 0, Jensen's alpha 5, beta 5. The total is 100 (computed here from the file).
  - Data checks flag, never repair: duplicate names, a stale NAV (older than 10 days against the newest NAV in the group), a category differing from the group's most common one, a 5-year CAGR with under 4.9 years of history, a 3-year CAGR with under 2.9 years, and impossible values.
