### inputs

### rules
State: observed
- Summary: Candidates are capped at `MAX_FUNDS_TO_SCAN` (60) alphabetically, not by quality. A fund whose NAV download fails is skipped with a warning. Duplicate scheme codes are dropped, stale funds are excluded, then eight hard filters run. Defaults in `config.py`: 3-year CAGR at least 10%, 5-year CAGR at least 10%, volatility at most 35%, history at least 5 years. The 1-year CAGR, risk-return, Sortino and rolling-positive filters are switched off (`None`). A filter drops a fund whose value is missing as well as one below the threshold. The five optional filters (AUM minimum and maximum, manager tenure, turnover, cash, top-10 concentration) are all off by default and never drop a fund that has no value; they flag the gap instead.

### Conclusion
- Does: Turns one or more fund categories into a ranked, filtered shortlist for the owner to read, by looking up Direct-Growth funds, downloading NAV history, computing metrics, filtering, scoring and writing four output files.
- Rules: Direct and Growth plans only (`config.py:25-26`); index funds, ETFs and fund-of-funds excluded (`config.py:27-28`); 60-fund cap per category (`config.py:29`); minimum 3-year and 5-year CAGR of 10% (`config.py:44-45`); maximum volatility 35% (`config.py:46`); minimum 5 years of history (`config.py:48`).
- On failure: exit codes 2, 1 and 0 as above; one failed download is skipped with a warning.
- Test result: tested on the surface. The end-to-end test asserts exit codes, output files, filter exclusions, duplicate handling, ranking order, LOW CONFIDENCE and the optional AUM filter. It uses fakes for the candidate lookup and the metric calculation, and the cap of 60 funds is not asserted.

## Supporting code

- `fund_universe.py` resolves a typed category to one of 12 canonical categories (`CATEGORY_ALIASES`, lines 33-48) and finds candidates by name keywords (`CATEGORY_KEYWORDS`, lines 52-65). "sectoral" and "thematic" share one category.
- `fund_filters.py` merges the optional attributes file and applies five optional filters, never dropping a fund for a missing value.
- `config.py` holds about 60 settings and loads `weights.ini` at import time (`apply_weights(WEIGHTS_FILE)`, line 182). `shortlist.py` changes `config.ROLLING_WINDOW_YEARS` at run time when `--rolling-years` is given (`shortlist.py:322`), so settings are shared mutable module state.
