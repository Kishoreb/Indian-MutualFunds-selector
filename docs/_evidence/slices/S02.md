### rules
State: observed
- Summary: Each metric returns `None` when history is too short, so a fund is scored on what it has. Rules found:
  - CAGR over 1, 3 and 5 years from the latest NAV back to the NAV on or before the start date; `None` if the fund's first NAV is more than 30 days after the start date. The 1-year figure is a simple return.
  - Volatility is the standard deviation of month-end returns times the square root of 12; needs 6 monthly returns.
  - Sharpe-style ratio is (CAGR minus risk-free rate) divided by volatility. The return leg is the 3-year CAGR, else 5-year, else 1-year. By default the risk side is measured over the same trailing period (`RATIO_ALIGN_WINDOW = True`). The Sortino ratio uses downside deviation instead.
  - Rolling returns are the trailing CAGR at every month-end; a fund needs 12 windows or the rolling statistics are `None`.
  - Maximum drawdown is measured over the last 3 years by default (`DRAWDOWN_WINDOW_YEARS = 3.0`), `None` if the fund is younger. The whole-history figure is kept as `max_drawdown_all`.
  - SIP XIRR simulates a monthly SIP of 1000 over 3 years, needs 6 instalments, and solves by Newton-Raphson with a bisection fallback.
  - Benchmark excess return is fund CAGR minus benchmark CAGR over matching dates (3 years); `None` if the benchmark's last date is more than 10 days older than the fund's. Rolling beat percentage is the share of matched rolling windows in which the fund beat the benchmark; needs 12 windows.
  - Downside capture is the mean fund return divided by the mean benchmark return in months when the benchmark fell; needs 6 down months. Lower is better.
  - Beta and Jensen's alpha use up to the last 36 monthly returns against the benchmark, need 24 common months, and count only when the benchmark is not staler than the fund. Both have weight 0 by default.
  - Quartile consistency is the share of month-ends on which the fund was in the top half of the funds in the same run; needs 8 funds on a date and 12 usable dates.

### Conclusion
- Does: Turns a fund's daily NAV history into the numbers the ranking is built from, and leaves a number empty when the history is too short, instead of guessing.
- Rules: see the rules facet. Thresholds: 30-day start tolerance, 6 monthly returns for volatility, 12 rolling windows, 6 down months, 24 months for alpha and beta, 8 peers and 12 dates for quartile consistency, 10-day benchmark staleness.
- On failure: short history gives an empty value; benchmark problems give a warning and empty benchmark values; a failed NAV download is handled by the caller (S01).
- Test result: well tested for the drawdown window, the aligned ratio window, alpha and beta, benchmark excess and rolling beat, downside capture and quartile consistency (known-answer assertions including the "too little data returns None" case). unknown for 1, 3 and 5-year CAGR, volatility and SIP XIRR in `metrics.py`: no assertion on those three was seen in the test files read (assertion lines only).
